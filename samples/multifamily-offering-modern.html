Property Overview
Physical Description

Two two-story garden buildings arranged around a central courtyard, built in 1974 on a half-acre lot, with two ground-floor storage areas converted to permitted accessory dwelling units in 2024.

Held by the same ownership since 2003 and operated with minimal rent increases, producing in-place rents approximately 15% below the surveyed submarket. Nine of eighteen units retain original kitchens and baths. Electrical remains original to construction and is sub-metered to each unit; water, sewer, and trash are owner-paid and currently recovered from residents at approximately 42% through a ratio utility billing system introduced in 2023.

Market Overview
Employment & Demand Drivers

A central San Fernando Valley location with employment anchored in healthcare, entertainment production, and light industrial — sectors that concentrate the moderate-income renter household this asset serves.

Major Employment Sectors

Healthcare Regional medical center, ambulatory surgical network, and skilled-nursing operators — the submarket's largest and most recession-resilient employment base.
Entertainment Production Post-production, sound stage, and equipment-rental facilities clustered along the Burbank and Studio City corridors, drawing crew-level renters valuing commute time.
Light Industrial & Logistics Warehouse and last-mile distribution along the Sepulveda and San Fernando Road corridors, supporting steady demand for workforce housing.
Municipal & Civic Government offices, courts, and a regional transit hub within a two-mile radius.

Sector descriptions are illustrative for this sample. A live engagement names specific employers with headcount and source.

Freeway Access

Interstate 405 within 1.2 miles; US-101 within 2.4 miles — direct access to the Westside and Downtown employment cores.

Transit

Regional bus rapid transit on the Van Nuys corridor with planned light-rail connection through the Sepulveda Pass.

New Supply

Limited multifamily deliveries within the immediate radius; new construction concentrated in larger transit-oriented parcels, not garden product.

Renter Profile

62% renter-household penetration against 46% county-wide, with median household income supporting the pro forma rent band.
